# drift-edge co-occurrence probes (2026-10-09)

question: are the two remaining affinity drifts real marketplace
crossovers, the way celestial -> gothic turned out to be ("celestial goth"
sold as a category)? or are they structural facts the engine is right to
keep apart? 300 probe listings, run ids and hashes in ../provenance.json.

## retro -> mid_century_modernist

real, and dealer-documented beyond the listings. robinsonsjewelers runs
a guide literally titled "the rise and fall of 'retro' mid-century
modern jewelry (1940s-50s)"; filigree jewelers and national jeweler
treat the 1940s as one continuous retro-to-mcm era. the probe: 45/100
ebay titles carry both retro-era and mid-century language, 28 of them
solimet-clean ("vtg gold tone artist paint palette brooch retro mid
century jewelry", "yellow enamel bib necklace brass retro mid century
statement jewelry"). "retro mid century" is normal seller phrasing.

## celestial -> art_deco

a real crossover category exists, but thinner and mostly stone-forward.
ebay: 31/100 both-titled, 15 solimet-clean - man-in-the-moon marcasite
deco brooches, deco moon hoops, "vintage celestial moon brooch black
onyx tone art deco jewelry". etsy: 38/100 both-titled but only 5
solimet-clean (the crossover there is gemstone rings). querit corrobates
the naming on poshmark ("antique art deco celestial crescent moon
rhinestone") and the 1930s trend press (vintagedancer). so sellers do
position deco celestial pieces as both - but the segment is
rhinestone/marcasite-heavy, i.e. adjacent to, not inside, the solimet
segment.

## caveat, honestly

co-titling rates overstate adjacency: sellers keyword-stuff the query
terms into titles. the probative items are the named exemplars above,
not the rates. querit raw passes (6 queries, search_ids in each file)
are in ../querit-2026-10-09/.

## disposition

both probes are evidence, not vocabulary changes. options for the
owner: (a) treat the retro->mcm co-titling as corpus-edge evidence and
record it in the vocabulary notes without facet changes (the honest
path - postwar_era already carries the transition); (b) for
celestial->art_deco, note the crossover exists but stays outside the
solimet segment, which supports leaving the drift as-is. no facet
changes are proposed here.
